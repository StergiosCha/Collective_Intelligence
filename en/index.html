Collective Intelligence, Democratic Control

Large Language Models and the Politics of Artificial Intelligence

Stergios Chatzikyriakidis and Themistoklis Pantazakos. Forthcoming in Greek from Εκδόσεις νήσος.

The book is written in Greek. This page gives a short summary in English.

The book defends a simple position that today's political debate has largely buried. Large language models are not alien entities to be feared and, ideally, abolished. They are alienated products of our own collective labour, which we must reclaim and are entitled to reclaim. The book is not against the machine. It is against the enclosure of the digital commons.
